THE FREE GUIDE

Find → Convert → Improve

A simple three-phase framework. You can begin with a notebook, a spreadsheet and a little consistent time.

01 / FIND

Find the right people

Start with your customer—not the social network everyone is talking about. Think about who needs your offer, who decides to buy, and where they look for help.

  • Describe two or three customer types and their problems.
  • Review five competitors and the questions their customers ask.
  • Make your services and contact details easy to find.
  • Choose one useful channel: search, social communities, referrals or direct outreach.
  • Publish helpful answers instead of only promotional messages.
Example · Local shop

Update opening hours and photos, answer common questions, and ask satisfied customers for honest reviews—without buying ads.

Try the persona builder →
02 / CONVERT

Give every enquiry a next step

When somebody contacts you, what happens next? A clear routine helps you respond consistently and stop overlooking opportunities.

  • Capture each enquiry and its source in one place.
  • Assign someone to respond—even if that's just you.
  • Ask questions to understand the person's needs.
  • Send a clear answer or quotation.
  • Set a reasonable follow-up date and record the outcome.
Example · Freelancer

Log every project enquiry, send a short discovery questionnaire, follow up once after a quote, and mark the result.

Build your sales routine →
03 / IMPROVE

Learn, correct, repeat

A lost opportunity isn't always wasted. Look for patterns in what customers ask, where you lose time, and why an offer isn't accepted.

  • Mark opportunities won, lost, postponed or not a fit.
  • Note the reason when you know it; avoid guessing.
  • Ask whether an existing customer needs additional help.
  • Improve one recurring issue each month.
  • Automate repeated tasks only once the process works.
Example · Small B2B supplier

Review lost quotes and notice that delivery details are often unclear. Add delivery information to every future quote.
